Placing your first bet

The bet slip works the same way across cricket, football, rugby and e-sports. Pick a market, the odds land on the right rail, and the stake field locks your potential return. A short primer on the main bet types:

  • Match winner (1X2). Single outcome — home, away, or draw. The default cricket and football slip.
  • Accumulator. Stack multiple selections on one slip. Odds multiply, but a single losing leg burns the whole stake. Sports bonus rollover binds to this type.
  • Handicap. Spreads the strength gap. SL favourite at -1.5 wickets needs to win by two; underdog at +1.5 wickets covers narrow losses and outright wins.
  • Over/Under. Predict total runs / total goals above or below a line. Half-line markets (e.g. O/U 165.5 in LPL) leave no push outcome.
  • Player specials. Top batter, top six-hitter, first wicket method, anytime tryscorer in rugby. These move fast — odds shift with team news.
